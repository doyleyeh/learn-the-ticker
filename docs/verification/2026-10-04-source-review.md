## Delivered behavior

Manual mode displays unchecked, registered source URLs with code-owned publisher/usage information before app evidence retrieval. Financial preparation resolves issuer scope without retrieving concepts, then fetches only the selected concepts. Filing indexes, documents and remaining narrative candidates have bounded review stages. Choosing sources does not bypass identity, exact quotation, numeric or current rights checks; unknown rights are not approvable. Original references accompany every admitted result. Identity lookup and permitted provider browsing remain governed by cloud research consent.

Reviews have a 120-second monotonic deadline, at most 100 options and 20 concurrent requests. Decisions are correlated, single-use and memory-only. Skipping grants nothing; cancellation/expiry/revocation/restore do not continue unanswered work. Review waits hold neither inference nor retrieval slots. Per-run review remains active after disabling the default setting; previously automatic in-flight evidence is withheld from later admission unless approved. Results use existing immutable storage; no dependency, source registry, provider permission, SQL/archive revision or packaging resource changes.
