# Independent research identity and rights checkpoint

Date: 2026-10-04. M2-T01a implementation checkpoint; **M2 and public v1 remain incomplete**.

## Scope and evidence

New production research resolves identity independently before inference where possible, then requires the complete proposed identity to agree before publication. Same-ID changes to issuer, exchange, share class/contract identifiers, type or currency cannot overwrite saved scope. An ambiguous independent result avoids inference. The initial SEC resolver reads the public exchange/ticker/CIK map; it has no fixed asset list and does not infer instrument type/currency. Missing listing metadata and unregistered categories remain unresolved. Independent all-category coverage is not established by synthetic category tests.

Document rules replace broad SEC/Investor.gov domain permissions. Rules separately record path, publisher/credibility, permission, review date/reference and freshness bound. SEC filing paths must match the independent CIK; a company-name mention in another issuer's filing cannot establish scope. Unknown/rejected/narrower permissions cannot cache raw text or provider-derived notes. Candidate dates, hashes, excerpts and attestation are cleared on manual/failure paths. Source/proof contracts reject embedded credentials and known authentication query parameters.

Online cache reuse requires matching asset, language and reader level, a fresh independent identity check, applicable document rights and underlying source dates. Latest requests recheck. A new bundle/retrieval timestamp cannot refresh old/undated information. Old snapshots remain immutable and available offline, with missing proof/level explicitly unknown. New proof/level fields survive validated backup/restore; proof hashes must match complete asset scope. The UI displays the saved reader level and an explicit identity failure message.

SEC automation requires an explicitly provided local contact, uses pinned public HTTPS addresses/TLS without redirects, bounds responses to 2 MB and shares a five-starts/second limit. The identity cache lasts one hour and failed requests have a cooldown. No contact is inferred from accounts or saved in library records. The explicit helper never invokes a provider, mutates a library or automatically retries. [DEC-023](../../DECISIONS.md#dec-023-bind-research-to-independent-identity-and-document-permissions) records the mechanism and rights rationale. No dependency, license, provider-policy or SQL revision change.
